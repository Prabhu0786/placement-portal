Before you start: your consent

To give feedback, your answers are sent to Google's Gemini AI service. Please read this first:

  • What is sent: the interview question, the answer you type, the interview type and your optional target role. Not your name, email, username or profile.

  • You are in control: you can delete any interview, or all of them, and withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Google handles the data under its own terms for the service plan in use.
  • AI feedback can be wrong or too generous. Treat it as advice and compare with the model answers.
