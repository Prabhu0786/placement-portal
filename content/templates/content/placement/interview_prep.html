How to answer well

  • Think first. A few seconds of silence is fine. Then answer in 4 to 8 sentences.
  • Behavioural questions: use the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result. Spend most of the time on what you did.
  • Technical questions: state the idea in one sentence, then explain with a small example. If you do not know, say so and describe how you would find out.
  • HR questions: be honest, specific and positive. Link your answer to the role.
  • Always: give one concrete example instead of general claims, and keep each answer to one or two minutes.
